--------------- POTS --------------------------------------------------- 
Postural Ortho Tachy Sd

POTS = chronic autonomic disorder defined by orthostatic symptoms with a sustained heart rate increase within 10 minutes of standing or head-up tilt, without orthostatic hypotension.

syst HR += 30 ( young >=40) withing 10 min стояния или tilt table
