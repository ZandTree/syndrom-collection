Если поц нестабилен, то назначь атропин и долбани дефибриллятором.

-- если нестабильна синусовая бради или Mobitz I ==> атропин 0.5-1 мг в/в с перерывом в  5  минут
   до разрешения симптомов или до общей (max) дозы атропина в 3 мг  
   Если атропин при  ==> pacing*

-- если у поца Mobitz II или third-degree AV block  ==> pacing*
Начни с 80 mA и reduce to the lowest energy that consistently initiates pacing.

Если не помог ни атропин, ни pacing ==> придётся:
           ---  epinephrine 20-50 µg IV bolus 
           ИЛИ 
           ---  epinephrine 2-10 µg/min IV infusion.
* ФБ источник: (check)
NOR-epinephrine: 8mg in 50ml NS infused as 6-30 ml/H (titrated to pt response)


                                Канадские профи:
                                ===============
If patient is unstable, initiate either atropine or transcutaneous pacing.
-- For unstable sinus bradycardia or Mobitz I, administer atropine 0.5-1 mg IV q3-5min until resolution or the maximum dose (3 mg) is reached.
-- If atropine is ineffective or in cases of Mobitz II or third-degree AV block, initiate transcutaneous pacing.
                                PEARLS
                                ======

If there is no response to atropine and/or transcutaneous pacing, administer epinephrine 20-50 µg IV bolus or epinephrine 2-10 µg/min IV infusion.

                                Transcutaneous pacing
                                =====================
1. Place pads on the patient’s chest at base and apex locations (or anterior/posterior).
2. Activate pacing.
3. Adjust energy delivery of the pacing stimulus.                               
Start at 80 mA and reduce to the lowest energy that consistently initiates pacing.
